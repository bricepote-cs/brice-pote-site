Core Skills

01

Functional scoping, design & project governance

  • Scoping & Specifications: Gathering of business requirements, clarification of use cases, writing and challenging of detailed functional specifications (SFD), technical specifications (TSD) and technical architecture documents (DAT).
  • Project management: Structuring of schedules, tracking of workload and budgets in man-days (consumed, remaining), identification of the critical path and alerts on drift.
  • Governance bodies: Preparation and facilitation of steering committees (COPIL), project committees (COPROJ), technical committees (COTEC) and change advisory boards (CAB); circulation of decision-oriented progress summaries.
  • Cross-functional coordination: Operational bridge between business departments (MOA), development teams, architects, security, operations teams and external providers.
02

TMA, provider & public procurement management

  • TMA governance: Management of dedicated teams in service centres, arbitration of capacity between handling the incident backlog (RUN) and delivering change requests (BUILD).
  • Contract management: Tracking of budget consumption in Work Units (UO), validation of monthly activity reports and checking of the invoicing statement in contractual committee.
  • Service commitments (SLA): Tracking of guaranteed intervention time (GTI) and guaranteed restoration time (GTR) by incident criticality (P1 to P3), on-time delivery rate (OTD). Handling of service deviations, requirement for root cause analyses (RCA) within 48h and negotiation of compensation.
  • Acceptance process & public procurement: Distinction and application of the contractual validation steps: Vérification d'Aptitude (VA), probationary period of Vérification de Service Régulier (VSR) and formal Admission decision for the services.
03

Acceptance testing, transition to RUN & lifecycle

  • Organisation of acceptance testing: Design of acceptance testing strategies, definition of test books, defect tracking and formalisation of acceptance reports (PV de recette) with a reasoned opinion.
  • Transition to RUN: Preparation of go-live files (DMEP), assessment of application impacts, verification of rollback procedures, writing of operations manuals (DEX) and organisation of knowledge transfers.
  • Application MCO & Obsolescence: Management of incident and service request flows, tracking of end-of-support schedules for application frameworks and platforms, and planning of version upgrades.
  • Digitalisation: Experience of electronic document management (GED), electronic archiving, electronic signature and electronic invoicing projects (public e-invoicing platform).
04

Methodologies, tools & technical foundation

  • Delivery methods: V-model with contractual milestones and Agile approaches (Scrum, Kanban); change management and familiarisation of teams with delivery rituals.
  • Management tools: Jira, Confluence, ServiceNow, ProjectLibre, Trello, Microsoft 365 office tools.
  • Automated indicators: Portfolio tracking dashboards, Grafana dashboards and operational alerts.
  • Technical understanding: Command of application architectures (PHP/Symfony, REST API, SQL, Docker containers, CI/CD), enabling effective dialogue with developers and challenging of architecture proposals.
